Bookend — Support

Help with the Bookend app for macOS, iOS/iPadOS, and Android.

Bookend is a local-first workspace for structured documents and notes. If something isn't working, or you have a question or a feature request, we're happy to help.

Email us at bookend@wayfarertechnology.org.

We read every message and aim to reply within a few business days.

Before you write

A few things often resolve an issue quickly:

  • Make sure you're on the latest version of Bookend from the App Store.
  • Fully quit and reopen the app.
  • Your documents are stored on your device — keeping a backup of important .bookend files (for example in the Files app or iCloud Drive) is always a good idea.

Reporting a problem

So we can help as fast as possible, please include:

  • Your device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18.1; or MacBook Air, macOS 15).
  • The Bookend version (shown in the app's About / settings).
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it helps show the problem.

Please don't send sensitive personal information in a support email. We only need enough to reproduce and fix the issue.
